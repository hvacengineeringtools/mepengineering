Formula

V= 16.5·Q·Pu·PiZmax·ΔP·Pc
dV= 16.5·QZmax
  • Qfactor × required flow, L/min
  • ΔPPu − Pi, bar absolute
  • PcPi − 0.5 bar, empty-tank charge
  • Vtank volume, litres

Pu is normally 0.5 to 1 bar above Pi. Check the permitted starts per hour with the pump and motor manufacturer. Service pressure is a vessel rating and is not used here.

Method: Boyle's law with a start-frequency limit. With the demand at half the pump flow, the shortest pump cycle sets the drawdown dV = 15 Q / Zmax (Q in L/min, Zmax in starts per hour), increased by 10 % to 16.5 Q / Zmax. The vessel volume is V = dV · Pi · Pu / (Pc · ΔP) with all pressures absolute. The pre-charge is taken 0.5 bar below the cut-in pressure. The selected size is the next size up in a typical nominal range; confirm it against the manufacturer's range and the vessel's pressure rating.
