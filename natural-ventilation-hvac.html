    6. Suggested selection

    Enter room loads. A blank fresh-air box is taken as 0. If a fresh-air flow is typed, that value is held and total supply is solved. Return = total − fresh, so mixed air changes with the suggested total.

    Suggested-selection chart

    Method: ASHRAE Handbook—Fundamentals, Chapter 1. Psychrometric properties from the shared file psychro.js (Hyland–Wexler saturation, over ice below 0 °C; ASHRAE wet-bulb equations; valid −20 to 80 °C). Streams are mixed on dry-air mass and the mixed dry-bulb is solved from h and W. Volume flows convert to dry-air mass with the specific volume of each stream at its own state. Enthalpy-consistent loads: Qs = ṁ (1.006 + 1.86 Wcold) ΔT and Ql = ṁ ΔW (2501 + 1.86 Twarm), so Qs + Ql = ṁ Δh. The coil line from mixed air to coil-off is extended to saturation to find the apparatus dew point; the contact factor is taken on dry-bulb. The enthalpy of the condensate is neglected in the coil load, which overstates it slightly. IP enthalpy is referenced to 0 Btu/lb at 0 °F dry air.
