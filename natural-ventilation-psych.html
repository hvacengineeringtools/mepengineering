    2. Two known properties

    Any two of dry-bulb, wet-bulb, RH and dew point. The rest, plus enthalpy and humidity ratio, follow.

    Below 0 °C this sheet follows ASHRAE and works over ice: RH is relative to saturation over ice, the wet-bulb is an ice-bulb and the dew point is a frost point. Weather stations usually give RH over water at all temperatures. At −5 °C, 80 % RH over water is about 84 % over ice, so convert a winter station reading before entering it.

    Method: ASHRAE Handbook—Fundamentals, Chapter 1. Saturation pressure by Hyland–Wexler, over ice below 0 °C; wet-bulb by ASHRAE Eqs. 33 and 35.
