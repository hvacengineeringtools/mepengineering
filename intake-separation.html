Use this sheet when the greater of the exhaust flow and the outdoor-air flow is above 0.5 m³/s. At or below 0.5 m³/s, use Table C.3.

Exhaust air category

EHA is the exhaust air after it leaves the building. The class is the pollution of that air, from Table 7. A higher class needs a greater distance from an outdoor-air intake.

ClassPollutionMeaning
EHA 1LowAir from rooms whose main sources are the building materials and structure, and from occupied rooms whose main sources are human metabolism and the building itself. Rooms where smoking is allowed are excluded.
EHA 2ModerateAir from occupied rooms that is dirtier than EHA 1, from those same sources and/or from human activity.
EHA 3HighAir from rooms where moisture, processes, chemicals, tobacco smoke or similar sources substantially reduce the air quality.
EHA 4Very highAir with odours and impurities in concentrations well above those allowed in an occupied zone. EHA 4 is to be discharged at roof level (9.2.5).

3. What h and L are

This drawing follows the small sketch printed beside Figure C.1: a stepped building in section, with the outdoor-air intake T on the middle step, an exhaust A₁ on the step above and an exhaust A₂ on the step below. h is the vertical distance and L the horizontal distance between the exhaust and the intake. Only the exhaust you chose is drawn. The distances are drawn to scale from your entries and redraw as you change them; the red dashed line is the minimum from Figure C.1. The drawing is redrawn for this site.

Horizontal and vertical together. Figure C.1 has the vertical distance on one axis and the horizontal distance on the other, so an exhaust that is both above (or below) and to the side is read directly: enter h and L, and the sheet reads the horizontal distance needed at your h and compares it with your L. Do not combine the two into one diagonal distance.

Openable windows. Clause C.1.3 speaks of ventilation intakes. Treating an openable window as an intake is a common, cautious reading; it is not stated in the standard.

4. Figure C.1

The marked point is this opening. A point on or to the right of its curve meets the figure. Each curve is a straight-line reading of Figure C.1, accurate to about ±0.2 m, and starts from the same distance in both panels. EHA 1 and EHA 2 need no horizontal separation once the exhaust is 2 m or more above the intake; when it is 4 m or more below, the requirement also falls to zero.
