Tower assumption (an estimate)

The effect of flow depends on the tower’s fill and size, which are not known here. The sheet assumes a typical open-circuit tower: an air-to-water ratio of 1.2 at the first case and a characteristic slope of 0.65 (handbook value −0.5 to −0.8). A different slope changes the answer by a few tenths of a kelvin. For a particular tower, use the maker’s data on sheet XV.
